Chords in 3D pi, base 4

Same picture as the flat chord diagram: the 4 symbols sit around a ring, and each pair of consecutive symbols is joined by a curve through the middle. Here every chord is also lifted to its position in the sequence: the first chord is at the bottom, later ones higher up, so the order becomes visible. Colour = the symbol the chord starts from. Left: pi; right: random digits (the control). If pi hid a pattern in time, the left tube would show bands, twists or gaps that the right one lacks. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
