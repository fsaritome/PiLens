half-integer z=1/2 lift with free b1: 6n^2 | -n(8n^3+pn^2+qn+r): 3D view

Every rule this hunt tried is a point in a box of whole-number settings (the grey frame). The coloured dots are the rules whose fraction turned out to equal a formula in π; with more than three settings, the colour shows a fourth one. Bright red crosses would be hits on the random control number (there should be none). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover a dot for its formula. The grey shadows on the walls are what the flat maps show: a 3D trail seen from one side.
